ABOUT

Designing the system and the story around it.

Creative Profile

I work across game design, systems thinking, narrative design, visual development and illustration. My portfolio includes fully documented concepts, branching systems, playable prototypes, comics and character-driven story development.

I am especially interested in turning themes into mechanics — making the idea of a project something the player can actually feel through interaction.
