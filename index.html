Creative Profile

I am a B.Tech Computation and Media student at Mahindra University working across game design, interactive storytelling, visual communication and creative technology. My work spans documented game concepts, branching systems, playable prototypes, comics, illustration, animation and character-driven narrative development.

I am especially interested in turning themes into actual stories and ideas; making an idea something the player can actually feel through interaction. Alongside game work, I have experience in performance and storytelling, motion graphics, student entrepreneurship, AI-assisted media workflows and campus technology communication.
