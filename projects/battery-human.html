Battery Human

A 4–6 player facility-escape horror concept built around one rule: the players are not collecting the resource. They are the resource.

PC3D first-personUnity / C# conceptCDD + GDD
THE PLAYER IS THE RESOURCE

High Concept

Players wake inside a research facility whose doors, terminals, generators, rescue pods and final exit are powered by human energy. Progress consumes parts of each player: Stamina, Warmth, Memory, Voice and Identity, creating a co-op horror loop where teamwork, sacrifice and betrayal all use the same economy.

If the facility needs a battery, who will you let it use?

Core Gameplay Loop

Explore the facility
→
Spend Human Charge on objectives
→
Evade the Warden & rescue teammates
→
Raise Drain Intensity
→
Negotiate the final sacrifice

The key escalation is that progress itself makes the facility more dangerous. Completed objectives increase Drain Intensity, so the early match is exploratory, the mid-game becomes coordination-heavy, and the endgame becomes desperate.

Human Charge System

Each resource is simultaneously currency, ability fuel and a condition that changes how the player experiences the game.

StaminaRunning, forcing doors and carrying teammates; low values slow movement and make chases harder.
WarmthPowers frozen systems and rescues; low values create cold visual feedback and weaker recovery.
MemoryUsed for hacking, maps and codes; low values remove or corrupt information.
VoiceUsed for calls, pings, distractions and voice locks; low values weaken communication.
IdentityUsed for scanners, overrides and the final exit; at low levels the facility stops recognizing the player as fully human.

Interlocking Systems

Shared Sacrifice

Group machines can split a required Human Charge total across several players, making contribution itself a social negotiation.

Drain Intensity

Four phases (Low Drain, Rising Drain, Emergency Lockdown and Full Consumption) escalate costs, Warden pressure and level restrictions.

Capture & Rescue

The Warden places captured players in Drain Pods. Rescuing them costs the rescuer’s own resources, turning altruism into a meaningful mechanical sacrifice.

Emergency Overrides

Shortcuts can transfer costs to another player, enabling betrayal without making betrayal mandatory.

Warden AI

The facility’s hunter responds to sound and objectives, then becomes more aggressive as Drain Intensity rises.

Multiple Outcomes

Team Escape, Solo Escape, Betrayal Escape, Everyone Drained and System Shutdown reflect how the group spent or weaponized its resources.

Player Experience Goal

The design aims for a “social horror story”: players should be afraid of the Warden, but also uncomfortable with the fact that helping the team has a personal cost. The desired emotions are fear, guilt, suspicion, panic, relief and shock, ideally producing matches where players negotiate, argue, rescue and occasionally sacrifice one another.
