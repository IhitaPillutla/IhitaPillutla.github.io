PSYCHOLOGICAL NARRATIVE

IDENTITÄT

A coming-of-age narrative adventure set in early-2000s Freiburg, Germany. After an identity-shattering incident and fragmented memory, the player does not recover a preset personality; they build one through behavior.

Identity as gameplay

Choices develop traits across Confidence ↔ Hesitation, Trust ↔ Suspicion and Kindness ↔ Defensiveness.

Memory fragments

Locations, people and emotions trigger distorted or symbolic recollections that gradually reconstruct the central incident.

Narrative consequence

“Punishment” is framed as reflection: relationships, dialogue and identity outcomes change according to repeated behavior.

Replayable identity

Resilient, Withdrawn, Defensive and Balanced outcomes make self-definition the progression system.

HORROR / RHYTHM

音楽教室 (Ongaku Kyoshitsu)

Set in a seaside Sendai high school in 1997, this horror/action concept turns rhythm into both survival mechanic and narrative threat. The “Ghost of Music” does not simply punish missed notes; it sabotages the performance itself.

Key swapping

The ghost can change input mappings mid-performance so memorization alone is unsafe.

Corrupted sheet music

Notes can shift, disappear or lie, pushing the player to trust listening as much as visual information.

No traditional combat

Survival comes from rhythm, adaptation and recovery rather than weapons.

No simple game-over

Mistakes alter the environment and narrative instead of only resetting the player.

ABSURD SOCIAL SURVIVAL

The Last Person in Line

A choice-based narrative mystery about joining a queue without knowing what it is for. The horror is not a monster; it is bureaucracy, sunk cost and the strange social pressure that makes everyone defend a system they complain about.

PositionHow close the player is to the front: physically, socially and psychologically.
PatienceWaiting, contradictory instructions and unfairness erode the player’s emotional stability.
FairnessTracks how the line judges the player; being too fair can trap them forever, while unfairness may help them progress.
RealizationsInstead of items, the player collects insights about how the queue works, unlocking stronger system-breaking choices.

Five endings include Still Waiting, The Cutter, The Front Was Empty, The Last Becomes First, and Everyone Leaves the Line.

“The most powerful person in a line may not be the first, but the last.”
